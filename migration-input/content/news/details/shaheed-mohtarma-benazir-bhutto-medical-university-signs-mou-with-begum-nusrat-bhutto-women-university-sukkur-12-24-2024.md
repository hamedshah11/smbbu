# Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University signs MoU with Begum Nusrat Bhutto Women University Sukkur

Dec 24, 2024

Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University signs MoU with Begum Nusrat Bhutto Women University Sukkur on Tuesday, 24 December, 2024 at BNBWU Sukkur. The vice chancellor Smbbmu Prof Nusrat Shah and Vice Chancellor BNBWU, Prof Tehmina Nangraj signed the MoU, pledging collaboration in the fields of academics, research and technology.
