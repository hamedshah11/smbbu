# The 50th Golden Jubilee Ceremony of Chandka Medical College

Feb 24, 2024

The 50th Golden Jubilee Ceremony of Chandka Medical College and its First Grand Alumni Re-Union was held on 24 February 2024 at the Latif Auditorium of Chandka Medical College. Prof Ali Muhammad Ansari, the first Principal of Chandka Medical College was the Chief Guest, and Ex Principals Prof Sikander Shaikh and Prof Akbar Haider Soomro were Guest of Honor. Prof Khalid Mehmood, Sitara-e-Imtiaz, renowned Meritorious Professor of Medicine, and alumnus of batch 3 was the Keynote speaker. He spoke on “Future of Medicine”.
