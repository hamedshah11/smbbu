# Meeting about Self-Institutional Performance Evaluation

Nov 19, 2020

As per the guidelines from HEC the Self-Internal Evaluations of SMBBMU was conducted in last 2 years by the Institutional Performance Evaluation (IPE) panel and now next step is to prepare the Implementation/ Action Plan Summary addressing the observations highlighted in IPE report.

Therefore, the Quality Enhancement Cell call a meeting of all concerned stakeholders to discuss and prepare the Implementation/ Action Plan Summary for subject mentioned report on ***Thursday 19th November, 2020 by 11:00 AM*** at Vice Chancellor Secretariat.
