# Free Mega Medical Camp in Celebration of Jashn-e-Azadi

Aug 07, 2025

As part of the Jashn-e-Azadi 2025 celebrations, a Free Mega Medical Camp was organized on Thursday at Taluka Hospital Warah, on the special initiative of Prof Dr Nusrat Shah, Vice Chancellor, SMBBMU, Larkana, in collaboration with Dr. Gulzar Ali Tunio (DHO Kamber Shahdadkot) and the Sindh Government Health Department.

A team of senior professors, consultants, and gynecologists from Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University (SMBBMU) provided free medical checkups and treatment for a range of conditions,

- Hepatitis
- Skin Diseases
- Malaria
- Tuberculosis (TB)
- General & Gynecological Checkups

All medical services, tests, and medicines  provided completely free of cost.
