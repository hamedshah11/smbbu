# Interactive talk on How to make a Successful application for achieving Chevening Scholarship UK

Oct 17, 2023

The Directorate of ORIC successfully organized a talk on "How to make a Successful application for achieving Chevening Scholarship UK"

The speaker for this session was Reema Salman Engagement Officer Communications Department, British High Commission, Lahore.

The postgraduate students from various colleges attended the informative talk on scholarships in the UK both in person and online.
