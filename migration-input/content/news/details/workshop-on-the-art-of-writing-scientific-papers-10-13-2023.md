# Workshop on The Art of Writing Scientific Papers

Oct 13, 2023

Under the visionary leadership of the Hon'ble Vice Chancellor, the Directorate of ORIC, SMBB Medical University Larkana successfully organized a workshop today on The Art of Writing Scientific Papers in the video conference room, CMC Larkana.

The speaker for this session was Dr. Yeboah Kwaku Opoku from Ghana.

Approximately 40 undergraduate students from various colleges attended the informative scientific workshop.
