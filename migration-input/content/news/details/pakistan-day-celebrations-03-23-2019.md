# Pakistan Day Celebrations

Mar 23, 2019

Pakistan day ceremony celebrated at Auditorium Hall @ Chandka Medical College Larkana. Ceremony was commenced with hoisting of National Flag  Vice Chancellor Prof Dr Aneela Atta Ur Rahman hoisted the National Flag.
