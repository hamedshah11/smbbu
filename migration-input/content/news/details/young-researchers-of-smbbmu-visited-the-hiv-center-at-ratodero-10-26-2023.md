# Young researchers of SMBBMU visited the HIV Center at Ratodero

Oct 26, 2023

Our young researchers visited the HIV Center at Ratodero on 26-10-2023 under the supervision of Director Oric Dr Mir Hassan Khoso, Director of Student Affairs Dr Fouzia Chandio, and Dr. Rehana Sadhayo lecturer Physiology Department. Students interacted with and interviewed the HIV children and their parents and counseled them about their treatment and rehabilitation.

Positively a great learning experience for students to get a glimpse into the real-life suffering of HIV patients in the community.
