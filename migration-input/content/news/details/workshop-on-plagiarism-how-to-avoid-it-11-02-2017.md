# Workshop on Plagiarism: How to avoid it

Nov 02, 2017

The Quality Enhancement Cell, SMBBMU Larkana in Collaboration with department of Medical Education organized One day workshop on ''Plagiarism: How to avoid it'' for Post Graduate Supervisors of SMBBMU Larkana on 2nd November 2017 at Video Conference Hall, SMBBMUL.

**Prof. Dr. Khurshid Ahmed Abbasi,** Ex-Chairman Plagiarism Standing Committee SMBBMU was Facilitator of this workshop.

  

**Facilitator :**  *Prof. Dr. Khurshid Ahmed Abbasi*
