# Notice for interviews of hafiz-e-quran candidates who have applied for admission for MBBS/BDS

Jan 06, 2021

**NOTICE**

All the candidates who have applied for admission to MBBS / BDS courses session 2020-21,  are hereby informed that the interviews for **HAFIZ-E-QURAN**persons shall be held on Thursday, the 1**4th of January 2021 on 10:00 AM**at Vice Chancellor's Secretariat, SMBBMU, Larkana for CMC tagged districts & Principal Office GMMMC, Sukkur for GMMMC tagged districts.
