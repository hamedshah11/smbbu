# Notice for Day One Celebration of Batch 48 at Chandka Medical College

Dec 28, 2019

This is for the information of all newly selected students of first year MBBS at Chandka Medical College Larkana Session 2019-20, That the Day One shall be celebrated on **Wednesday** The 1st of **January** **2020**, **11:00 AM** in Auditorium Hall Chandka Medical College, Larkana.

All newly selected students are invited to attend along with their parents. Shields to meritourius students from all districts shall be awarded by worthy Vice Chancellor of Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University Larkana.
