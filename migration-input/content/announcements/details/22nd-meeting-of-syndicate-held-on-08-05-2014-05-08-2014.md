# 22nd Meeting of Syndicate held on 08-05-2014.

May 08, 2014

Prof. Ghulam Asghar Channa, Vice Chancellor Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University Larkana visited various Departments of CMC Larkana on Tuesday, 15-04-2014. He held surprise round of Examination Halls at Chandka Medical College Larkana during the examination conduction procedure. Latteron he visited Examination Branch of University where Prof. Saifullah Jamro, Controller of Examinations (Under-graduate) gave the briefing. Thereafter, His Excellency proceeded to the University land and perceived the on-going development works. In the last on-site briefing was given by Project Director in which he submitted details of projects.
