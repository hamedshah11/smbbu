# Introduction

![Featured image 1](https://www.smbbmu.edu.pk/beta/uploads/employees/images/1693390139.jpeg)
  
**Dr. Ramchand Thadhani**

Dr. Ramchand Thadhani is a primary care physician in Galloway, NJ, and is Chairman of the Department of Medicine Atlantic Regional Medical Center Atlantic City, NJ. He received his medical degree from Liaquat Medical College Sindh and has been in practice for 22 years. He also specializes in hospice & palliative medicine and is experienced in the United States Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality.
