# Introduction

![Featured image 1](https://www.smbbmu.edu.pk/beta/uploads/employees/images/1693242233.jpg)
  
**Dr. Aziz Ahmed Soomro**

Dr. Soomro is a trained Child, Adolescent, Adult, and Interventionist Psychiatrist. Dr. Soomro completed his Adult Psychiatry training at NYU School of Medicine, North Shore University Hospital Manhasset New York. Dr. Soomro subsequently completed his Child and Adolescents Psychiatry training at Dartmouth Medical School. Dr. Soomro has been affiliated with Brookdale Hospital, Westchester Medical Center, Chappaqua Behavioral Medicine, St Vincent Hospital, and Northwell Health . Dr. Soomro has been involved in teaching and supervision of resident and fellows in training. Dr. Soomro has extensive experience in treating children, Adolescent and Adults.

Dr. Soomro specializes in psychiatric evaluations, medication management, psychotherapy, telepsychiatry, Ketamine infusion, Sparavato and Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation.
