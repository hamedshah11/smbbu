# Introduction

![Featured image 1](https://www.smbbmu.edu.pk/beta/uploads/employees/images/1692684019.jpg)
  
**Dr. Khalil A. Khatri**

Khalil A. Khatri, M.D. is a Harvard trained Dermatologist and Cosmetic Surgeon, specializing in cosmetic and dermatological laser treatments. His personalized approach has made him one of the most well-respected and in-demand dermatologists in the northeast.

Dr. Khatri has over 25 years of experience with cosmetic lasers and is internationally renowned for his research in laser technology, particularly Erbium: YAG laser treatments to improve the appearance of photo-damaged skin. He has served as the Medical Director at various aesthetic laser companies, leading the research and development of ground-breaking technologies. He is a sought-after speaker & educator, presenting at both national and international meetings where he trains other physicians on cutting-edge laser technologies.
