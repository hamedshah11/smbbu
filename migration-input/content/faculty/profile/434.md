# Introduction

![Featured image 1](https://www.smbbmu.edu.pk/beta/uploads/employees/images/1692683805.jpg)
  
**Dr. Rizwan C. Naeem**

Dr. Rizwan C. Naeem is a highly accomplished academic-oriented physician executive with more than 25 years of experience in the fields of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine. He has held significant roles in prestigious academic medical centers in the United States, including Baylor College of Medicine, Tufts University, and Albert Einstein College of Medicine.

# Education

- Clinical and Research Fellow in Molecular Genetics/Division of Genetics, The Children’s Hospital Boston and Brigham’s and Women Hospital, Harvard Medical School, Boston, MA, 1990 to 1993
- Fellow in Clinical Cytogenetics at The Center for Human Genetics, Boston University School of Medicine, Boston, MA, 1988 to 1990
- MBBS (MD): Medicine 7’s Bachelor and Bachelor of Surgery, Sindh Medical College University of Karachi, Pakistan, 1979-1985
