# Introduction

![Featured image 1](https://www.smbbmu.edu.pk/beta/uploads/employees/images/1693289706.PNG)
  
**Dr Javed Suleman**

For over 21 years, Dr Javed have been a practicing Clinical and Interventional Cardiologist associated/employed by Mount Sinai Hospital. His interests have been focused on development of Primary Angioplasty Program without surgical back up (CPORT) which he successfully completed as Principal Investigator at Elmhurst Hospital Center in 2003 and at Jamaica Hospital Medical Center in 2008. To note Elmhurst Hospital Center was the first hospital in the New York Metropolitan area to start such a lifesaving program which was developed under his leadership. Since completing his Interventional Cardiology Fellowship from Mount Sinai Program in 2000, Dr Javed continued his affiliation with Mount Sinai Cardiac Catheterization Laboratories performing around 200 coronary interventions year after year. He had been a Full Time Faculty with Mount Sinai Hospital, New York and Medical Director of Mount Sinai Cardiology Satellite office in South Richmond Hill, Queens, New York since 2008. He participate in teaching Interventional and Clinical Cardiology Fellows at Mount Sinai Cardiac Cath Lab. He work 4 days a week as a Clinical Cardiologist and Medical Director at Mount Sinai Doctors office in South Richmond Hill, NY and one full day a week performing cardiac catheterizations and interventions at Mount Sinai Cardiac Catheterization lab, since 2008. Dr Javed also strongly believe in contributions towards social welfare and giving back to the community, which takes me to Karachi, Pakistan, his native town, a couple of times annually, volunteering his time to perform coronary interventions on indigent non-affording patients. The Government of Pakistan kindly acknowledged his volunteer services and contributions in the field of Cardiology and awarded him with the prestigious Tamgha-e-Imtiaz (Medal of Distinction) in 2009. Since graduating from Interventional Cardiology Fellowship in 2000, Dr Javed had been consistently serving the low socioeconomic area of Queens as Clinical and Interventional Cardiologist. During the peak of the COVID-19 pandemic in April / May 2020 when there was dire shortage of healthcare providers, Dr Javed volunteered for two weeks at Mount Sinai Hospital and for three weeks at Jamaica Hospital Medical Center taking care of patients with severe COVID infection.

As Principal Investigator of the site, developed program of “Primary Angioplasty without surgical backup” at Elmhurst Hospital Center, through C-PORT Registry. As Principal Investigator of the site, developed program of “Primary Angioplasty without surgical backup” at Jamaica Hospital Medical Center, through C-PORT Registry. Principal Investigator for CRUSADE study at Elmhurst Hospital Center (2003), and Jamaica Hospital Medical Center (2003 to 2006), (Can Rapid Risk Stratification of Unstable Angina Patients Suppress ADverse Outcomes with Early Implementation of the ACC/AHA Guidelines).
