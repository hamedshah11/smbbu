# College of Medical Technology Larkana

# Introduction

The College of Medical Technology at SMBBMU, Larkana is a dynamic initiative
and dedicated to establish the future of healthcare by equipping students with
advanced knowledge, technical expertise, and the professional competencies
required to excel in modern medical practice.

Medical technology is the application of devices, procedures, and knowledge for
monitoring, diagnosing and treating disease for the purpose of maintaining,
promoting, and restoring wellness while improving the quality of life.
The BS in Medical Technologies Innovation and Development program
cultivates the advanced skills and strategic expertise necessary to drive the
development, commercialization, and global market integration of cutting-edge
medical technologies.

Learners will not only develop critical research skills to drive innovation in
medical technologies but will also gain a deep understanding of regulatory
requirements and advanced testing methodologies, enabling them to
strategically plan and successfully introduce new technologies to the global
market.

Graduates from our college are in high demand among opportunities in hospitals,
clinics, research centers, diagnostic laboratories, and various healthcare
organizations. With our exceptional faculties with highly esteemed professionals
with extensive qualifications and experience, advanced facilities, and diverse
range of programs, you can be inspired to turn dreams into reality and elevate
yourself beyond every expectation.

**Vision**  
To contribute to society through the pursuit of quality education, research and Clinical
services and to produce competent and skilled human resource for serving the health
needs of local national and global communities.

**Mission**  
The College of Medical Technology SMBBMU Larkana strive to prepare graduates who
contribute to safe, accurate and evidence based medical services, strengthen the health
care system and address the emerging diagnostic needs of community and beyond.
The College of Medical Technology is committed to:

1. Produce distinguished undergraduate medical technologists through an advanced
   educational training and research oriented environment.
2. Achieve the international standards in teaching and learning through the evidence
   based knowledge and quality of education.
3. Providing competency based education that strengthens and practical skills for
   safe and compassionate patient care.
4. Interprofessional collaboration with SMBBMU teaching hospitals and partner
   institutions to ensure high quality in healthcare and community.
5. Promote an inspiring and supportive educational environment that facilitates
6. graduates to use their potential to achieve national priorities and professional
   excellence.
