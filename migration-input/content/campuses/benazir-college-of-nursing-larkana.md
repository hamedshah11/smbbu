# Benazir College of Nursing

# Introduction

Benazir College of nursing is a unique and premier nursing Institute, established in the year 2012 is one of the constitute college of Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University Larkana, and is recognized by Pakistan Nursing Council and Higher Education Commission of Pakistan. It is one of the leading institutes that incorporate all the guidelines and principles set by the council and commission.  
Benazir College of Nursing is leading nursing college in interior Sindh accommodating students from all over the country as per seat allocation. The graduates of BCON are serving at attractive wages in different public and private sector organizations, according to a partial requirement of undergraduate students study survey conducted in 2019 on illumines of BCON, the result shows that employment ratio of graduates is almost 100%(99.00).
The expenses of Students of BCON are being covered with multiple scholarship programs e.g HEC Need Base Scholarship, Merit Top ten, Bait ul mal, District wise scholarships, Ehsas Program and NEST scholarships.
BCON provides an equal opportunity to students for mental, physical and professional development through different curricular and co-curricular activities. Students have been provided an opportunity to participate in conferences, seminars, Debates, speeches, sports competitions on District, province and National Level and have been enlighten the institutes with many positions.

# Principal Message

![](https://www.smbbmu.edu.pk/beta/uploads/employees/images/1768935130.jpeg)

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to Benazir College of Nursing, a constituent college of Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University (SMBBMU), Larkana. Our college is committed to preparing competent, ethical, and compassionate nursing professionals who can meet the evolving needs of the healthcare system.
Benazir College of Nursing offers undergraduate and postgraduate nursing programs, supported by a team of well-qualified and experienced faculty, modern teaching facilities, and a conducive academic environment. We place strong emphasis on clinical competence, professional ethics, and continuous learning, supported by adequate infrastructure and learning resources.

Our academic and research direction aligns closely with the visionary leadership of Professor Dr. Nusrat Shah, Worthy Vice Chancellor, SMBBMU, whose focus on strengthening healthcare through quality education, research, and innovation continues to guide our institutional goals. In line with this vision, the college actively promotes research culture, evidence-based practice, and scholarly engagement among faculty and students.
We strive to produce nursing graduates who demonstrate strong clinical skills, critical thinking, ethical practice, leadership qualities, effective communication, and a commitment to lifelong learning. Our graduates are prepared to serve with professionalism, empathy, and responsibility in diverse healthcare settings at national and international levels.
At Benazir College of Nursing, we believe that education is a transformative journey. We are dedicated to nurturing nurses who not only provide quality care but also contribute meaningfully to research, education, and the overall improvement of the health system.

**Nazeer Ali Buriro**
  
**Principal**
  
Benazir College of Nursing
  
SMBBMU, Larkana
